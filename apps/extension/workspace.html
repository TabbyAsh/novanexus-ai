YOUR SOURCES

Use the Lens toolbar to scan the page you’re viewing. Capture sold-result rows separately.

Open-page observation updates every 5 seconds while visible. Optional BID13 background refresh targets 5 minutes; browser suspension and source limits can delay it.
01 / OBSERVE

Start with a real source.

Open a product or auction, click the Lens toolbar, then scan. No inventory or market value is assumed.

These settings track evidence. They never place a bid. Other sites update from your open source tab.

02 / APPRAISE

Show the evidence behind the price.

Each check confirms that you reviewed model, condition, quantity and final sale price for the selected item. Active asking prices, undisclosed offers, missing dates and old evidence cannot establish a sold range.

Capture sold comparisons

Open eBay’s sold-results view for the exact model. Use Lens → Scan current page. Return here and confirm the relevant rows. For other marketplaces, use an authorized source export in a Lens backup; unsupported feeds are not connected automatically.

03 / DECIDE

Full costs. Explicit unknowns.

Acquisition, transport, shipping and all other costs

Uses the existing Auction Desk engine. Deposit is tied-up cash; labor is an economic cost. Missing costs block a ceiling. A sourced historical range is a scenario, not a guaranteed sale.

04 / WATCH

Changes worth revisiting.

Stored event history, not push delivery. A countdown reaching zero does not prove the auction sold. Later outcomes remain separate from the original forecast.
