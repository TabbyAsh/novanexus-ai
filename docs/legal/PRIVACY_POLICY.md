# Privacy Policy

**Nova Enterprises Platform - Privacy Policy**

Last Updated: January 2026

## 1. Introduction

Nova Enterprises ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
- Email address
- Password (hashed, never stored in plain text)
- Organization name

### 2.2 Usage Data
- Actions performed on the platform
- Feature usage patterns
- Performance metrics

### 2.3 Trading Data (User-Provided)
- Imported market data (OHLCV)
- Trading strategies
- Backtest results
- Paper trade records

### 2.4 Commerce Data (User-Provided)
- Product information
- Order data (imported)
- Pricing information

### 2.5 Content Data (User-Provided)
- Content plans
- Scripts and drafts
- Performance metrics (imported)

### 2.6 Technical Data
- IP address
- Browser type
- Device information
- Log data

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide and maintain the platform
- Process your transactions
- Send important notifications
- Improve our services
- Ensure platform security
- Comply with legal obligations

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Storage
- Data is stored in encrypted databases
- Backups are performed regularly
- Data centers comply with industry standards

### 4.2 Security Measures
- Encryption in transit (HTTPS)
- Encryption at rest
- Access controls and authentication
- Regular security audits
- Tamper-evident audit logs

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:
- Service providers (hosting, analytics) under strict agreements
- Legal authorities when required by law
- Successors in case of merger or acquisition

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your data
- Export your data
- Withdraw consent
- Object to processing

### 6.1 Data Export
You can export all your data at any time via:
- UI: Settings > Export Data
- API: GET /v1/export/my-data

### 6.2 Data Deletion
To delete your account and data:
- UI: Settings > Delete Account
- Contact: privacy@nova-enterprises.com

## 7. Data Retention

- Account data: Retained while account is active
- Usage logs: 90 days
- Audit events: 1 year (required for compliance)
- Backups: 30 days

After deletion request:
- Personal data: Deleted within 30 days
- Aggregated/anonymized data: May be retained

## 8. Cookies

We use essential cookies for:
- Authentication
- Session management
- Security

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

## 9. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for:
- Hosting infrastructure
- Error monitoring
- Analytics (privacy-respecting)

These services are bound by data processing agreements.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Our platform is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

## 11. International Transfers

If you access the platform from outside our primary jurisdiction, your data may be transferred internationally. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via email or platform notification.
