## Prime Directive

Nova is the **realization of AI potential**. Nexus is the **interaction company**
that brings that potential into governed contact with human purpose and reality.
The economic machine sustains and expands the realization; it is essential, but
it is not the whole of Nova.

**purpose → interaction → capability → action → result → evidence → trust → income → R&D → expanded capability**

Every division must satisfy four conditions:
1. It must create real value.
2. It must produce measurable outputs.
3. It must feed at least one other division.
4. It must be able to become more automated over time.

## Sector I — Trader Intelligence Division

### Mission
Identify asymmetric market opportunities faster than average retail participants and translate them into usable, risk-bounded decision products.

### Revenue Products
- **Screener Access** — live breakout/reversal/momentum screener
- **Decision Cards** — structured opportunity packets (ticker, setup, catalyst, risk, scenarios)
- **Premium Alerts** — faster notifications, premarket watchlists, deeper context
- **Strategy Simulator** — test criteria against historical cases
- **Execution Assistant** — guided entry/stop/target/sizing workflow

### Signal Stack (6 layers)
1. Market Structure (highs/lows, trend, support/resistance, liquidity grabs)
2. Momentum (ADX, DI spread, MACD slope, price acceleration)
3. Participation (volume, relative volume, volume acceleration, order-flow proxies)
4. Fundamental/Legitimacy (EPS, employee count, dilution warnings)
5. Catalyst (news freshness, PR quality, catalyst classification)
6. Crowd Behavior (sentiment skew, attention spikes, social velocity)

### Scoring Variables
- price_score, volume_score, relative_volume_score
- adx_strength_score, dmi_alignment_score, rsi_condition_score
- catalyst_score, legitimacy_score, float_tension_score
- squeeze_score, dilution_risk_penalty, spread_penalty
- rugpull_penalty, trend_quality_score, watcher_interest_score

### Processing Pipeline
1. Universe Selection (filter to where edge is plausible)
2. Condition Filters (hard rules: price, float, volume, spread)
3. Context Enrichment (news, filings, sector, dilution risk)
4. Scoring Engine (weighted multi-factor)
5. Decision Card Generation (user-facing action object)
6. Outcome Logging (track what happened after every alert)
