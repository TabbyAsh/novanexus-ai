## 🔐 Risk Management & Mitigation

### Enterprise-Level Risks

| Risk Category | Description | Mitigation Strategy |
|---------------|-------------|---------------------|
| **Regulatory** | Changes in financial, data, AI regulations | Dedicated compliance team, legal counsel, proactive engagement |
| **Technology** | System failures, security breaches | 99.99% uptime SLA, security audits, disaster recovery |
| **Market** | Trading losses, market downturns | Risk limits, position sizing, diversification |
| **Competition** | Larger players entering space | Product differentiation, community building, speed |
| **Talent** | Key person dependencies | Equity incentives, documentation, succession planning |
| **Capital** | Insufficient funding | Multiple funding sources, path to profitability, unit economics |
