Privacy

This page describes the inquiry forms on novanexus-ai.com. It is a plain account of what those forms collect and why. Nova Enterprises LLC is in Terre Haute, Indiana. The contact is Wyatt Kirby, wkirby592@gmail.com, 812-263-1586.

The site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (including IP address and browser type) under its own policy. Inquiries are stored in a hosted Postgres database (Neon). No analytics or advertising cookies are used.

What the forms collect

  • Sourcing quote: name, company, email, product, quantity and unit, and a request id. Phone, country, target date, and notes if you enter them.
  • Supplier inquiry: name, company, email, country, whether you manufacture or intermediate, product range, and your permission to be contacted. Phone, minimum quantity, and notes if you enter them.
  • Services inquiry: name, email, which service (Researched B2B lead lists (contacts published on company websites), government bid research, or a note about software and AI, which is in development), and a description of the request. Company and phone if you enter them.
  • A hidden field that must stay empty. If it is filled, the submission is not stored.
  • A hash of the network address, kept only to limit repeated submissions. The inquiry record does not store the raw address.

Why

The details are stored so Wyatt can read the inquiry, reply, and keep a business record with a reference id (for example NOVA-20261029-AB12). A second submit with the same request id does not create a second record. The forms are not sent to FormSubmit.

An email to wkirby592@gmail.com is intended after the record is saved. That email is turned off until an email provider is approved. The saved record does not depend on the email being sent.

If the form cannot save

If the database is not available, the salt used to hash network addresses is missing, or the save fails, nothing is stored. The next page says: We couldn't save your request. Please email wkirby592@gmail.com or call 812-263-1586.

Who can see it

The public site does not list inquiries. The owner queue is password-protected on the server. Nova does not sell these details. A CSV export exists for the owner and uses the same records.

Inquiries are deleted automatically 24 months after they are received. Network-address hashes are deleted after 90 days.

Asking about your information

Email wkirby592@gmail.com to ask for a copy or for a deletion. Include the reference id if you have one.
