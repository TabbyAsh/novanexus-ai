How sourcing works

From your brief to a written quote.

You send the specification. Written quotes come back. You decide whether to continue.

  1. 01

    Send your spec
    and quantity.

    Tell us the product, materials, quantity, and target date. Include branding or packaging requirements if you have them.

  2. 02

    We gather written
    quotes.

    Quotes come back in writing with the specification, price, and terms a mill offered. Samples are requested only when you want them and a mill can provide them.

  3. 03

    You decide what
    happens next.

    Nothing is committed until you approve the written terms. Order coordination happens only after that approval.
